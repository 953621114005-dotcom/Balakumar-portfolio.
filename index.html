About

A designer with an engineering mindset.

I approach design the way I was trained to approach engineering problems — by understanding the system before touching the surface. Before I design a screen, I want to know what task it's helping someone complete, and why the current way of doing it isn't working.

That habit comes from spending my days around inspection data, quality records, and operational processes. It's given me a grounded sense of how real workflows behave, which now shapes how I think about dashboards, forms, and flows as a designer.

"Good design is not only about how an interface looks. It is about how clearly it helps someone complete a task."
